# Functional metadata
- **Coût du risque**: sum of impairment/ECL charge over the requested period, in EUR. Default: trailing 12 completed months. Present absolute EUR and, when available, basis points versus average EAD.
- **Coefficient d exploitation**: operating expenses / net banking income * 100. Threshold in the demo: strictly greater than 60%.
- **Concentration top 20**: EAD of the 20 largest clients divided by total EAD, within month, country and sector.
- **ECL**: scenario-weighted expected credit loss under demo IFRS 9 logic: PD * LGD * EAD * scenario weight, summed across scenarios.
- **Charges allouées**: amount transferred from a cost pool to an entity/product using a named driver. Explain variance as pool-size effect, driver-volume effect and mix/rate effect.
- **Currency**: EUR after normalization.
- **Synthetic data**: all clients, entities and figures are fictitious.
